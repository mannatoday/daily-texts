How do I turn this website into a phone app?

Add it to the home screen so the family devotion is quick to open each day.

You already opened this from the home-screen icon.

This browser can install it directly.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this site in Safari.

  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (a square with an arrow pointing up).

  3. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

  4. Name it 摩拉維亞每日箴言, then tap Add at the top right.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this site in Chrome.

  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.

  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

  4. After you confirm, a shortcut that looks like an app appears on the home screen.
