關於 Moravian Daily TextsAbout Moravian Daily Texts

Moravian Daily Texts 自 1731 年開始出版，是歷史最悠久、持續出版的每日靈修讀本之一。每天包含一段舊約經文、一段新約經文、禱告及讀經進度，陪伴全球信徒以神的話開始每一天。

Moravian Daily Texts has been published since 1731. Each day offers an Old Testament watchword, a New Testament text, and a plan for reading Scripture.
